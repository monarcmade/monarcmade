## What Monarc University actually is

Monarc University is a guided AI builder cohort.

Not a prompt library. Not a course you buy and never open. A structured path that takes you from zero to a real, shipped AI project.

It is built around six phases:

- AI foundations.
- Prompt systems.
- AI APIs and apps.
- RAG and knowledge systems.
- Agents and automations.
- Production plus capstone.

Every phase pushes toward something you actually build, not just watch.

By the end, you do not just understand AI better. You have a working capstone project, a repeatable workflow system, and a demo you can use for business, career growth, content, or client work.

## Why this needs to exist

AI is not short on information.

That is the problem.

Every week there is a new model, tool, tutorial, shortcut, automation, agent platform, or "10 prompts you need" post. You can spend months collecting content and still not know what to learn next.

Monarc University is designed to give you the order.

You learn the foundations first, then prompt systems, then workflows, then how AI connects to apps and business data. Agents come later, after the workflow is clear enough to trust.

That sequence matters.

## Two live paths, same outcome

You can move through the live curriculum at two different paces.

The **12-Week Fast Track** is for people who want speed and structure. It includes two live sessions per week, weekly labs, reviews, and a capstone shipped by week 12.

The founding cohort price is **$1,997**. A payment plan is available at **3 payments of $750**.

The **24-Week Guided Cohort** covers the same six phases at a lighter weekly commitment. It includes one live session per week, more room to practice, more review time, and the same capstone outcome.

The founding cohort price is **$2,997**. A payment plan is available at **6 payments of $550**.

Both tracks end at the same place: a shipped AI project you can point to.

## Self-paced and VIP options

Not everyone needs the same level of support.

The **Self-Paced Starter** is **$297** and includes recorded lessons, templates, starter prompts, and project examples for students who want to move independently.

The **VIP Build-With-Me** path starts at **$5,000** and is scoped around a defined AI project you plan to sell, deploy, or use inside your business.

The live cohort is the core offer. Self-paced gives people a lower-cost starting point, and VIP gives serious builders a more hands-on path.
